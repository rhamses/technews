will render its HTML content without including a <code>&lt;!DOCTYPE html&gt;</code> declaration nor any <code>&lt;head&gt;</code> content.</p>\n<p>Partials are best used in conjunction with a build times for sites with many images.</p>\n<p>Additionally, Astro will now reuse the same buffer for all variants of an image.
