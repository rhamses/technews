Astro makes it easy to ship only what you need - 100% static HTML by default, bring your own framework to sprinkle in interactivity only where you need it.</p>\n<p><strong>Today, we’re excited to announce two new catalogs to help speed up development: <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://astro.build/themes/\">Themes</a> and <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://astro.build/integrations\">Integrations</a>.</strong></p>\n<img src=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2Fastro-themes-integrations.CeHE7OfW.webp&w=1536&h=866&f=webp\" srcset=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2Fastro-themes-integrations.CeHE7OfW.webp&w=768&h=433&f=webp 768w, https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2Fastro-themes-integrations.CeHE7OfW.webp&w=1500&h=846&f=webp 1500w\" alt=\"Astro Themes and Integrations Catalog\">\n<p>With our new <strong>Theme Catalog</strong>, it’s never been easier to go from idea to live traffic. And when it’s time to add your favorite tools, libraries and services into Astro, our <strong>Integration Catalog</strong> has got you covered. Extend Astro with a single <code>astro add</code> command.</p>\n<h2 id=\"introducing-astro-themes\">Introducing Astro Themes</h2>\n<p>Astro has always maintained a collection of official <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://github.com/withastro/astro/tree/main/examples\">example projects</a> and <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://astro.new/\">starter templates</a>. These were great learning resources, but they were also limiting: 1 official blog theme, 1 official docs theme, etc. etc.</p>\n<p>Meanwhile, our amazing community of developers had already begun to build and share fully-designed themes on our community Discord. Do you keep meaning to start a personal blog, but never seem to find the time? Grab a copy of the <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://github.com/one-aalam/astro-ink\">Astro Ink</a> theme and start writing! With built-in support for dark mode, automated publishing for draft posts, and client-side search, you’ll skip weeks of hacking to jump straight into the sharing your content.</p>\n<p>We created the Astro Themes Catalog to showcase these amazing community-developed themes alongside our official set of starter kits.</p>\n<img src=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2Fastro-themes.DA1px1lx.webp&w=1536&h=866&f=webp\" srcset=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2Fastro-themes.DA1px1lx.webp&w=768&h=433&f=webp 768w, https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2Fastro-themes.DA1px1lx.webp&w=1500&h=846&f=webp 1500w\" alt=\"Astro Themes Catalog\">\n<p>Visit <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://astro.build/themes/\">astro.build/themes</a> to get started with any official or community theme.</p>\n<p>Interested in releasing your own theme? We’re here to help! Check out our <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/publish-to-npm/#packagejson\">publishing best-practices</a> for help getting started and instructions to get your theme listed on our catalog. Need help? Join the #themes channel on <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://astro.build/chat/\">Discord</a> to chat with other Astro theme creators.</p>\n<h2 id=\"integrations\">Integrations</h2>\n<p>With your frontend out of the way, how do you actually use your favorite CMS, or add analytics to the site? Our <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://astro.build/blog/astro-025/#new-astro-integrations\">recently announced</a> Integrations API makes it possible to bring your own frameworks, CSS tools, and packages into Astro with just a few clicks. To browse everything available, our new <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://astro.build/integrations\">Integrations Catalog</a> makes finding the right integrations for your next project a breeze.</p>\n<p>For example, let’s say you’re having performance trouble with 3rd-party scripts on your page. This isn’t surprising, since <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://web.dev/web/fundamentals/performance/optimizing-content-efficiency/javascript-startup-optimization/\">sending too much JavaScript</a> can lock up the main thread and block the <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://developer.mozilla.org/en/docs/Web/API/GlobalEventHandlers/onload\"><code>window.onload</code></a> event (even if the script is marked as <code>async</code> or <code>defer</code>). Search our Integrations catalog to find the <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://github.com/withastro/astro/tree/main/packages/integrations/partytown\">official Partytown integration</a> for Astro, and add it with a single command:</p>\n<img src=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2Fastro-partytown.Do4LsdID.webp&w=1536&h=864&f=webp\" srcset=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2Fastro-partytown.Do4LsdID.webp&w=768&h=432&f=webp 768w, https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2Fastro-partytown.Do4LsdID.webp&w=1500&h=844&f=webp 1500w\" alt=\"Astro + Partytown\">\n<p>Check out the <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://docs.astro.build/en/reference/integrations-reference/\">full docs</a> for details on how to build your own integrations.</p>\n<h2 id=\"ship-it\">Ship It</h2>\n<p>We can’t wait to see what you come up with! <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://github.com/withastro/astro.build/issues/new/choose\">Add your own themes to the catalog</a>, publish your own <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/publish-to-npm/#integrations-library\">components and integrations</a>, and join our <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://astro.build/chat/\">Discord</a> to say hello!</p>\n</article>"
