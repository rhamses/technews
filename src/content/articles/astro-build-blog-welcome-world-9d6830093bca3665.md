contentHtml: "<article>\n<p>For just over three years, Astro has been on a mission to empower the web development community to build a better web. We have undergone plenty of changes over the years, most recently with the release of <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://astro.build/blog/astro-2\">Astro 2.0</a>. Today, we are proud to unveil a new look and feel for Astro!</p>\n<video autoplay controls loop muted><source src=\"https://site-assets.astro.build/blog/launch-video.mp4\" type=\"video/mp4\"><p>Sorry, your browser doesn’t support embedded videos.</p></video>\n<h2 id=\"the-journey-awaits\">The Journey Awaits</h2>\n<p>Astro’s brand has always been rooted in the idealism of the early internet. Being a web developer was the ultimate super power. For the first time in human history, you could create and share digital experiences around the world instantly.</p>\n<img src=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2F1.iTJxjbi9.png&w=1536&h=801&f=webp\" srcset=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2F1.iTJxjbi9.png&w=768&h=401&f=webp 768w, https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2F1.iTJxjbi9.png&w=1500&h=782&f=webp 1500w\" alt=\"\">\n<p>Over the last three years we’ve been through several iterations of our website and visual style. Our first website was just a single-page manifesto of the Astro vision and a hidden <code>console.log</code> easter egg to join a secret early-access Discord.</p>\n<p>We’ve come a long way since then, and Astro deserves a visual look-and-feel that reflects our vision for the next three years. We partnered with Jessica Streiloff and her team at <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://www.strelioff.com/\">Streiloff &amp; Co</a> to help us create a new look for Astro that felt modern without losing that idealism of the early web.</p>\n<p>For three months we went on a journey to understand what Astro meant to us. We interviewed our community, including core maintainers and power users of Astro. We looked at our past and our future. After digesting all the feedback that they had gathered, Jessica’s team came back to us with a storyboard concept that really clicked.</p>\n<h2 id=\"ready-for-takeoff\">Ready For Takeoff</h2>\n<p>Our updated brand has several components. Let’s take a closer look at each one.</p>\n<h3 id=\"logo--mark\">Logo &amp; Mark</h3>\n<p>The logo only required minor refinements, such as adjusting the shape of the mark and improving the type for better readability. For example, the previous mark was visually difficult to center. The new logo improves on this with some lighter manipulations and spacing updates.</p>\n<img src=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2F3.DMQt6WtF.png&w=1536&h=801&f=webp\" srcset=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2F3.DMQt6WtF.png&w=768&h=401&f=webp 768w, https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2F3.DMQt6WtF.png&w=1500&h=782&f=webp 1500w\" alt=\"\">\n<h3 id=\"type-system\">Type System</h3>\n<p>Typography is something that we deeply care about within our team. We chose typefaces that are optimized for legibility but also reflect the vibrant personality of our brand. We chose OH no Type Company’s <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://ohnotype.co/fonts/obviously\">Obviously</a> as our main font for its versatility and bold personality. As a developer-focused platform it was also essential that we select an excellent code typeface, so we went with MDIO from <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://www.mass-driver.com/\">Mass-Driver</a>.</p>\n<img src=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2F4.67CqfbPd.png&w=1536&h=801&f=webp\" srcset=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2F4.67CqfbPd.png&w=768&h=401&f=webp 768w, https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2F4.67CqfbPd.png&w=1500&h=782&f=webp 1500w\" alt=\"\">\n<h3 id=\"visual-elements--color-palette\">Visual Elements &amp; Color Palette</h3>\n<p>We emphasized the importance of not looking like every other devtool out there when it came to the aesthetics of our brand. Dark-mode websites are very “in” right now, but we chose to pair one with a unique color palette and original illustration style.</p>\n<img src=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2F5.DQQG87uf.png&w=1536&h=801&f=webp\" srcset=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2F5.DQQG87uf.png&w=768&h=401&f=webp 768w, https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2F5.DQQG87uf.png&w=1500&h=782&f=webp 1500w\" alt=\"\">\n<h2 id=\"introducing-houston\">Introducing: Houston</h2>\n<p>If you’ve been following Astro for the last few months, you’ve probably already been introduced to Houston, our friendly Astro mascot. He’s been featured recently in everything from our experimental <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://houston.astro.build/\">AI assistant</a> to our redesigned <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://twitter.com/matthewcp/status/1617959618285633536\">error dialog</a> inside of Astro itself.</p>\n<p>What you might not know is that we discovered Houston almost by accident. Nate Moore had added an original “blob” to our <code>npm create astro</code> helper CLI to add a bit of personality to an otherwise boring “getting started” tool. Jessica at Streiloff &amp; Co loved this, and pushed us to think of the little fella’ as more than just a one-off.</p>\n<p>We worked through several iterations of Houston before we found a personality that reflected how we saw Astro itself: whimsical, approachable, and friendly.</p>\n<img src=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2F6.BIn4DTIl.png&w=1536&h=801&f=webp\" srcset=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2F6.BIn4DTIl.png&w=768&h=401&f=webp 768w, https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2F6.BIn4DTIl.png&w=1500&h=782&f=webp 1500w\" alt=\"\">\n<p>Houston may seem a bit square, but his visual simplicity is his strength (much like Astro itself!). With such a flexible foundation, Houston can be dressed up for any occasion or emotion:</p>\n<img src=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2F7.mse3pT2q.png&w=1536&h=801&f=webp\" srcset=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2F7.mse3pT2q.png&w=768&h=401&f=webp 768w, https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2F7.mse3pT2q.png&w=1500&h=782&f=webp 1500w\" alt=\"\">\n<h2 id=\"see-you-out-there-astronaut\">See You Out There, Astronaut</h2>\n<p>We hope you enjoy this rebrand as much as we do! As we continue to evolve, so will this look and feel. Stay tuned for more deep dives and explorations of the Astro brand.</p>\n<p>We hope to make our design system fully open-sourced in the future. In the meantime, to download our brand assets you can head over to our new <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://astro.build/press/\">press page</a>.</p></article>"
