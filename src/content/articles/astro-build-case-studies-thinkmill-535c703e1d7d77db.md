They have no project managers and are instead made up of a collective of designers and developers that like working together. Under this structure, it’s not uncommon for folks in different disciplines to be working together on the same parts of a project - even if it’s not their forte. Herein lay Thinkmill’s major challenge.</p>\n<p>The team wanted designers and content editors to be able to autonomously contribute to the codebase. However, their previous tech stack used Next.js because the team liked React. This made it difficult for non-developers and less experienced developers to contribute. As a result, updating the website required the team to enlist the help of a senior developer. This would take the developer out of their flow state and into an entirely different context - one that would take time for them to gather enough data on to deliver the work. It would also halt the work of whichever person was initially responsible for the work because they needed to get unblocked.</p>\n<h2 id=\"requirements\">Requirements</h2>\n<p>In order to maintain a website that was easy to use for both less experienced developers as well as folks that were not familiar with code, Thinkmill needed a solution that was simple.</p>\n<figure><img src=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2Fastro-benefits.D_QSi3rE.webp&w=1536&h=656&f=webp\" srcset=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2Fastro-benefits.D_QSi3rE.webp&w=768&h=328&f=webp 768w, https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2Fastro-benefits.D_QSi3rE.webp&w=1536&h=656&f=webp 1536w\" alt=\"\"><figcaption>Astro is extremely well documented and has a friendly community.</figcaption></figure>\n<p>Simon Swiss, Developer Advocate at Thinkmill, strongly advocated for the team to use Astro because it was extremely well documented. Anyone that wanted to jump into the website could easily upskill themselves to start contributing. Non-senior developers could manage the site with only HTML and CSS while senior developers who favoured React could feel at home with the JSX syntax. Astro also came with highly desirable content features such as MDX support, syntax highlighting and pagination out of the box so Thinkmill did not have to build those features.</p>\n<figure><svg width=\"1.28em\" height=\"1em\" /><blockquote><p>“Astro seemed to hit that sweet spot of simplicity of use without compromising on the power it\ngives to developers. This made it absolutely necessary for the solution to be flexible in case they needed to swap it out later on. Astro allowed Thinkmill to support their legacy MDX files straight out of the box. At the same time, if Thinkmill ever wanted to move to a headless CMS or even use an API, they could.</p>\n<p>Astro ended up being such a simple and flexible tool that the team made it a permanent solution.</p>\n<figure><img src=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2Fthinkmill-homepage.DpbTm_Cm.webp&w=1536&h=813&f=webp\" srcset=\"https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2Fthinkmill-homepage.DpbTm_Cm.webp&w=768&h=407&f=webp 768w, https://astro.build/_image?href=%2F_astro%2Fthinkmill-homepage.DpbTm_Cm.webp&w=1536&h=813&f=webp 1536w\" alt=\"\"><figcaption>Thinkmill homepage.</figcaption></figure>\n<h2 id=\"solution-and-implementation\">Solution and Implementation</h2>\n<p>When asked what the two most advantageous things about Astro were, Simon cited strong documentation and ease of entry for non-senior engineers.</p>\n<p>According to Simon, Astro was easy to explain to both sides. React engineers found Astro familiar because of the JSX syntax and it wasn’t that much of a reach for developers that only knew HTML and CSS. Astro felt like using HTML with JSX capabilities, which the entire team loved.</p>\n<p>The team heavily leveraged Astro components in their site - treating it almost like a React project but without any of the complexity that React can bring. As soon as <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/content-collections/\">content collections</a> released, Simon’s team started using them. Content collections even allowed Thinkmill to pair Astro with <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://keystatic.com/\">Keystatic</a> - their content management tool - to make content collections editable in a GUI.</p>\n<figure><svg width=\"1.28em\" height=\"1em\" /><blockquote><p>“Astro is a game changer for cross-functional teams.
