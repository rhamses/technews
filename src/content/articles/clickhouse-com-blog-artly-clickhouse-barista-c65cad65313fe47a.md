What’s exponentially harder, Tong says, is making 10,000 great ones, across dozens of locations, in real time. \"If you want every cup to be perfect, you have to make a lot of adjustments,\" he says.</p>\n<p>That’s where Artly’s data infrastructure comes in. Each barista bot collects hundreds of metrics, which the team relies on to deliver consistently great coffee at scale.</p>\n<p>The system runs on a hybrid cloud architecture across AWS and GCP. Each robot connects to a centralized backend where telemetry, operational logs, and sales data are stored in services like DynamoDB, S3, Elasticsearch, and ClickHouse.
