Data flowed from websites via a lightweight script into a Golang backend, which transformed the events and pushed them through AWS Kinesis into a Postgres cluster on Azure.</p>\n<p>“This setup worked decently when we had lower data volumes,” Mayank says. “But as we started to grow and scale, the cracks began to show.”</p>\n<p>Auditzy’s old Postgres-based architecture: sluggish, fragile, and overworked:\n<span><img alt=\"495743692-3d1f8dca-1c10-4a53-9eac-959666f29300.png\" src=\"https://clickhouse.com/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2F495743692_3d1f8dca_1c10_4a53_9eac_959666f29300_56f7ae6437.png&w=2048&q=75\"></span></p>\n<p>One of the biggest issues was query latency, especially with large datasets. “Any time we ran a query on a 100- or 200-million row dataset, it would take not just seconds, but minutes,” he says. “For a product that’s pitching real-time insights, this was not ideal.”</p>\n<p>Ingestion wasn’t any better. Bulk inserts slowed to a crawl during traffic surges, and every attempted fix—caching mechanisms, partitioning, materialized views, even custom aggregation systems—only added complexity. “All those workarounds created technical debt and meant we had to write a lot of code in order to maintain performance,” Mayank says.</p>\n<p>On top of that came the daily grind of maintenance. Postgres needed regular babysitting to manage index bloat and vacuum operations. As a result, new feature development dragged, bogged down by long test cycles and endless performance tuning.</p>\n<p>“With all these growing challenges, our efforts were kept more on the maintenance side, rather than building the product itself,” Mayank says. He knew they needed a better database—one <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://clickhouse.com/resources/engineering/how-to-choose-a-database-for-real-time-analytics-in-2026\">purpose-built for speed, scale, and real-time analytics</a>.</p>\n<h2 id=\"enter-clickhouse-the-perfect-fit\"></h2>\n<p>Auditzy’s journey to ClickHouse began in 2023, when Mayank came across a <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://zerodha.tech/blog/logging-at-zerodha/\">blog post by Zerodha</a>. When Auditzy started running into its own scaling problems, he decided to see if ClickHouse might be the answer.</p>\n<p>His first step was a proof of concept.
