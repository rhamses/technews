ClickHouse community is undeniably growing and it became a common database product to give a talk about or at least compare or refer to, while just <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://clickhouse.com/blog/blog/click-house-at-percona-live-2017\">two short years ago</a> it was more like “wth is ClickHouse?”.</p>\n<p>Alexey Rubin from VirtualHealth compared two column-oriented databases: ClickHouse and MariaDB Column Store. Also Altinity was sponsoring the ClickHouse booth in Expo Hall which became an easy spot for people interested in ClickHouse to chat outside of talks.\n<span><img alt=\"2019-percona-live-3.jpeg\" src=\"https://clickhouse.com/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2F2019_percona_live_3_1e5bfcf029.jpeg&w=2048&q=75\"></span></p>\n<p>Ruoxi Sun from PingCAP introduced TiFlash, column-oriented add-on to TiDB for analytics based on ClickHouse source code. Basically it provides <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://clickhouse.com/docs/en/engines/table-engines/mergetree-family/mergetree\">MergeTree</a>-like table engine that is hooked up to TiDB replication and has in-memory row-friendly cache for recent updates.
