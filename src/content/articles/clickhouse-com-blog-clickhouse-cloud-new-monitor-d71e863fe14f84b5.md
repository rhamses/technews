Platform administrators sit at the center of this experience: they tune scaling controls, configure server settings, and ultimately own the health of their deployment. On ClickHouse Cloud, we're releasing a set of improvements designed to give deeper visibility into how the ClickHouse server is behaving to proactively surface warnings before they become problems.</p>\n<h2 id=\"user-content-more-dashboards\"></h2>\n<p>The ClickHouse Cloud console already comes built-in with several monitoring dashboards. Designed to give administrators an at-a-glance health check of their ClickHouse environment, you can now spend less time hunting for information and more time acting on it.</p>\n<h3 id=\"user-content-infrastructure-page-deeper-scaling-visibility\"></h3>\n<p>The new Infrastructure page gives administrators a clearer view into how their services are scaling over time. CPU and Memory utilization metrics, now with additional aggregation types, show how much utilization your ClickHouse cluster is getting.</p>\n<p>New modals showcase ClickHouse's <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://clickhouse.com/docs/manage/scaling\">automatic scaling</a> behavior to help users better understand why and how their cluster scaled, and also make better decisions around custom scaling configurations like vertical scaling limits and idling behavior.</p>\n<p><span><img alt=\"\" src=\"https://clickhouse.com/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2Fclicklens_mar2026_image1_621e99285e.png&w=2048&q=75\"></span></p>\n<h2 id=\"user-content-get-ahead-of-issues-with-new-notifications\"></h2>\n<p>When users onboard onto ClickHouse, a common set of issues frequently come up.
