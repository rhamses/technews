contentHtml: "<div><p>Pub/Sub is the primary messaging layer for a wide range of GCP services: Cloud Logging, Cloud Monitoring, GKE workloads, and custom event producers across the GCP stack. As more teams route their GCP infrastructure logs, application events, and operational signals to ClickHouse Cloud, we wanted to lower the barrier to get this data in.</p>\n<p>Today, we're announcing a new <strong>GCP Pub/Sub connector for <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://clickhouse.com/cloud/clickpipes\">ClickPipes</a></strong>, now available in Private Preview! You can seamlessly stream data from GCP Pub/Sub into ClickHouse Cloud in <strong>real time</strong> with a few clicks and <strong>no additional infrastructure</strong>. The connector supports all <strong>common formats</strong> (JSON, Avro, Protobuf) and <strong>schema registry integration</strong>, with attribute-based message filtering, flexible seek options, and per-key ordered delivery. Like all ClickPipes connectors, it can also be managed programmatically via OpenAPI and the ClickHouse Terraform provider.</p></div><div><h2 id=\"why-build-a-clickpipe\"></h2>\n<p><strong>Simpler than building your own pipeline.</strong> Previously, getting data from Pub/Sub into ClickHouse meant routing through GCS or standing up a Dataflow pipeline. The Pub/Sub ClickPipe handles all of this automatically: point it at your topic, configure your target table, and the connector manages subscription lifecycle, schema inference, and message delivery for you.</p>\n<p><strong>Built for continuous ingestion.</strong> ClickPipes consumes messages in real-time, optimizing ingestion performance out-of-the-box and eliminating trade-offs between freshness and cost. The connector scales horizontally and vertically for high-throughput topics, is optimized for ClickHouse Cloud's architecture, and handles failures gracefully with configurable replicas and automatic retries.</p>\n<p><strong>Fully managed and native to ClickHouse Cloud.</strong> Built-in metrics, monitoring, and detailed logs give you visibility into your pipeline without standing up external tooling. You can edit ClickPipes in place (<em>e.g.</em> add columns, adjust ingestion settings) without recreating them from scratch.</p>\n<h2 id=\"main-features\"></h2>\n<h3 id=\"integration-with-schema-registry\"></h3>\n<p>All common serialization formats are supported: <strong>JSON</strong>, <strong>Avro</strong>, and <strong>Protobuf</strong>; including native integration with the <strong>Pub/Sub Schema Registry</strong>. Schemas and field types are inferred and mapped automatically to a target table with ClickHouse native data types. Compressed payloads are <strong>detected and decompressed automatically</strong>, with no additional configuration needed.</p>\n<h3 id=\"configurable-start-offsets\"></h3>\n<p>The starting offset supports Pub/Sub's native <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://docs.cloud.google.com/pubsub/docs/replay-overview\">seek feature</a>, giving you precise <strong>control over where ingestion begins</strong>: latest, earliest, or a specific timestamp for backfills and point-in-time replay for failure scenarios.</p>\n<h3 id=\"message-filtering\"></h3>\n<p>During ClickPipe creation, you can configure a Pub/Sub <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://docs.cloud.google.com/pubsub/docs/subscription-message-filter\">subscription filter</a> on message attributes to drop messages before they are pulled from the subscription, reducing ingestion volume and associated costs.</p>\n<h3 id=\"terraform-and-open-api-support\"></h3>\n<p>For mature ClickPipes deployments using workflow automation and Infrastructure as Code (IaC), a new <code>pubsub</code> source type is available in <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://clickhouse.com/docs/cloud/manage/api/swagger#tag/ClickPipes\">the ClickPipes OpenAPI specification</a>, as well as the ClickHouse Terraform Provider (<a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://github.com/ClickHouse/terraform-provider-clickhouse/releases/tag/v3.16.0\">3.16.0+</a>).
