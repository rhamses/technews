Traces are trees of span with nanosecond timestamps. Log patterns are clusters of identical messages that tell you <em>something</em> is happening 113,526 times but won't tell you <em>why you should care</em>. The gap between raw telemetry and human understanding is where SRE time goes to die.</p>\n<p>Today we're shipping <strong>AI Summarize</strong> -- a new feature in HyperDX that generates narrative summaries of your logs, traces, and log patterns. It works on any event in the side panel and on the pattern drawer. One click, and your cryptic span becomes a story.</p>\n<h2 id=\"what-it-looks-like\"></h2>\n<p>Open any log entry, trace span, or log pattern in HyperDX. You'll see a new <strong>Summarize</strong> button below the event body. Click it, and after a brief analysis you get something like this:</p>\n<p><span><img alt=\"april_fools_ai_clickstack_1.png\" src=\"https://clickhouse.com/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2Fapril_fools_ai_clickstack_1_112d4f71ce.png&w=2048&q=75\"></span></p>\n<p>Or maybe you'll get David Attenborough narrating your checkout flow:</p>\n<p><span><img alt=\"april_fools_ai_clickstack_2.png\" src=\"https://clickhouse.com/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2Fapril_fools_ai_clickstack_2_2b7d270393.png&w=2048&q=75\"></span></p>\n<p>Or Shakespeare lamenting your latency:</p>\n<p><span><img alt=\"april_fools_ai_clickstack_3.png\" src=\"https://clickhouse.com/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2Fapril_fools_ai_clickstack_3_01357825e5.png&w=2048&q=75\"></span></p>\n<p>Hit <strong>Regenerate</strong> to get a new version. The theme adapts to context -- errors get Detective Noir, performance issues get Shakespearean Drama, and normal info events get the Nature Documentary treatment.</p>\n<h2 id=\"it-knows-your-stack\"></h2>\n<p>The summaries aren't random mad-libs. AI Summarize understands the OpenTelemetry and Kubernetes attributes already present in your telemetry -- service names, versions, HTTP endpoints, database systems, RPC calls, exceptions, pod names, namespaces, durations, and more. It reads what's there and weaves it into the narrative.</p>\n<p>A 1ms Redis call gets <em>\"extraordinarily swift -- the peregrine falcon of API calls, diving at breathtaking speed.\"</em> A checkout span that errors out with a cache exception gets <em>\"Then I found the body -- the kind of exception that ends careers and starts postmortems.\"</em> A 5-second database query gets <em>\"an age! Methinks the user doth grow weary, staring at the spinning wheel of fortune.\"</em></p>\n<p>The mood adapts too. Errors and exceptions trigger darker themes. Warnings get suspicious. Healthy spans get the nature documentary they deserve.</p>\n<p>For <strong>log patterns</strong>, the summary incorporates the repeat count -- because a message that appears 113,526 times deserves to be called out:</p>\n<p><em>\"Hark! A refrain most persistent: 'EmptyCartAsync called with &lt;</em>&gt;'. 113,526 times it echoes through the cluster, like a chorus that hath forgotten how to stop.\"*</p>\n<h2 id=\"zero-tokens-zero-data-sharing-zero-cost\"></h2>\n<p>Here's the part we're most proud of: <strong>AI Summarize doesn't use any LLM.</strong> There is no API call to OpenAI, Anthropic, or any other provider. No tokens are consumed. Your data never leaves the browser.</p>\n<p>The summaries are generated entirely on the client side using hand-written phrase pools and OTel-aware data extraction. The \"AI\" in \"AI Summarize\" stands for \"Artisanally Improvised.\"</p>\n<p>This means:</p>\n<ul>\n<li><strong>No cost</strong> -- works on every HyperDX deployment, open source or cloud, with no AI API key required</li>\n<li><strong>No privacy concerns</strong> -- event data stays in your browser tab, never sent to a third-party model</li>\n<li><strong>No latency</strong> -- the ~2 second \"analysis\" delay is theatrical, not computational</li>\n<li><strong>No hallucinations</strong> -- every fact in the summary comes directly from your event attributes</li>\n</ul>\n<p>Click the <code>(i)</code> icon next to any summary to see the disclosure.
