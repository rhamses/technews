QuickCheck, with the mission of providing financial services to underserved consumers, leverages artificial intelligence to offer app-based neo-banking products.</p>\n<p>The QuickCheck mobile app has been downloaded by more than 2 million people and has processed over 4.5 million micro-credit applications. The team of 150+ people is located between Nigeria and Portugal.</p>\n<h2 id=\"clickhouse-for-multiple-use-cases\"></h2>\n<p>QuickCheck started using ClickHouse 2 years ago for multiple use cases including financial data analysis, fraud analysis, and monitoring data. Hundreds of thousands of rows of data are loaded into ClickHouse daily. On top of this data we perform analysis of portfolio risk and build the financial metrics needed for portfolio analysis.</p>\n<p><span><img alt=\"quickcheck1.png\" src=\"https://clickhouse.com/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2Fquickcheck1_bc35b189ad.png&w=2048&q=75\"></span></p>\n<p><span><img alt=\"quickcheck2.png\" src=\"https://clickhouse.com/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2Fquickcheck2_31cc6c8054.png&w=2048&q=75\"></span></p>\n<p>ClickHouse is also used for our operational dashboards. We collect tens of thousands of data points from customers’ phones and other more traditional sources. ClickHouse is used as a way to process all of these SMS messages and extract valuable information used for the scoring and fraud models.</p>\n<p><em>I love ClickHouse because the team that manages it says</em> – <em>it just runs, magically</em>, <em>it’s amazing</em></p>\n<h2 id=\"clickhouse-architecture\"></h2>\n<p><span><img alt=\"quickcheck3.png\" src=\"https://clickhouse.com/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2Fquickcheck3_63e0038fa7.png&w=2048&q=75\"></span></p>\n<p>The above diagram shows QuickCheck’s current ClickHouse architecture. Some people use machine learning models for data science and fraud detection. They connect directly with SQLAlchemy.</p>\n<p>What is also important to mention is that ClickHouse is a column-oriented database that doesn’t support transactions and updates/deletes are very slow. All transaction data should be kept in Postgres (or another OLTP database) and ClickHouse should be used for what it does best: OLAP queries. However, we are excited about the <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/issues/22086\">transaction support</a> experiments released in <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://clickhouse.com/docs/en/whats-new/changelog/\">22.4</a> and look forward to experimenting.</p>\n<h2 id=\"instant-in-clickhouse-vs-forever-in-postgres\"></h2>\n<p>For me, what matters about ClickHouse is the sheer performance it has. That is basically the reason why we started using it, for dashboarding.</p>\n<p>We realized we had so much data in Postgres, which was taking forever to process, so we started moving it to ClickHouse. It is instant in ClickHouse vs forever in Postgres.</p>\n<p>If I try to do a financial analysis of the last year it’s impossible to do in Postgres, the database is going to time out, it will never finish. In ClickHouse it takes less than 5 seconds.</p>\n<h2 id=\"clickhouse-features\"></h2>\n<p>The main features of ClickHouse we currently use are:</p>\n<ul>\n<li>Aggregation functions (AVG, SUM, etc) across 100s of millions of rows to create dashboards</li>\n<li>Window and statistical and functions</li>\n<li>Column compression to save on disk space usage</li>\n</ul>\n<h2 id=\"advice-to-other-clickhouse-users\"></h2>\n<p>My advice to others thinking of using ClickHouse is to just try it.
