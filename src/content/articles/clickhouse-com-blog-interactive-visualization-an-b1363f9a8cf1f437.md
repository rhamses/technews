Our co founder and CTO Alexey Milovidov has built an interactive visualization and analytics tool on this data. If nothing else, he's invented an entirely new art form in the process!</p>\n<p><span><img alt=\"newyork.png\" src=\"https://clickhouse.com/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2Fnewyork_4b4291b34f.png&w=2048&q=75\"></span>\n<a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://adsb.exposed/?zoom=12&lat=40.7168&lng=285.9893&query=e18e8c8d6a1db73c63953798ad8919a9\">Helicopters over Manhattan</a></p>\n<p>This website aggregates and visualizes massive amounts of air traffic data. The data is hosted in a<a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/\"> ClickHouse</a> database and queried on the fly. You can tune the visualizations with custom SQL queries and drill down from 50 billion records to individual data records. The result is some pretty spectacular imagery!</p>\n<p>While this blog post mainly focuses on how the demo was built, feel free to <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://clickhouse.com/blog/interactive-visualization-analytics-adsb-flight-data-with-clickhouse#awesome-visualizations\">skip to the end</a> for some jaw-dropping visuals.</p>\n<p>Alternatively, visit <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://adsb.exposed/\">https://adsb.exposed</a>, find the visual for your local city, and share it on social media! This protocol is unencrypted, and there are no restrictions on collecting, using, or redistributing this data. Most passenger airplanes are obligated to broadcast this data, and even gliders, drones, and airport ground vehicles in certain countries. Military and private light aircraft might or may not broadcast.</p>\n<p>It is possible to collect this data out of thin air using your own radio receiver (e.g., in the form of SDR), although your receiver will see the data only in a specific range of your location. There are platforms for sharing and exchange of this data. Some platforms invite participants to share the data but restrict its redistribution by providing commercial access. While the source data, broadcast by airplanes, is essentially public domain, the companies may produce and license derivative works from this data.</p>\n<p>We use the data from two sources: <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://www.adsb.lol/\">ADSB.lol</a> (complete historical data is provided without restrictions: 30 to 50 million records per day, available since 2023 under the <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://github.com/adsblol/globe_history_2024/blob/main/LICENSE-ODbL.txt\">Open Database License</a>) and <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://www.adsbexchange.com/products/historical-data/\">ADSB-Exchange</a> (only provides samples of data from the first day of each month: around 1.2 billion records per day with better coverage).</p>\n<blockquote>\n<p>Another promising data source, <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://airplanes.live/\">airplanes.live</a>, has been brought to our attention. The author offered to provide historical and real-time data for non-commercial usage. It has great coverage and data quality, and we are going to include it in the following days.</p>\n</blockquote>\n<h2 id=\"implementation-details\"></h2>\n<p>The website is implemented as a single HTML page.
