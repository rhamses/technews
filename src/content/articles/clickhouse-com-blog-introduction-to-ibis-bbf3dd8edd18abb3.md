A diagram is shown below:</p>\n<p><span><img alt=\"Intro to Ibis.png\" src=\"https://clickhouse.com/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2FIntro_to_Ibis_996e3e461f.png&w=2048&q=75\"></span></p>\n<p>In the diagram, Ibis is the user interface.
