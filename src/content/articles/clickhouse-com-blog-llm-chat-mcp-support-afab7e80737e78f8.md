This showcases LibreChat's extensible architecture and cutting-edge protocol support, making it ideal for sophisticated, data-driven AI applications beyond simple chat interfaces.</p>\n<p>You can run LibreChat directly with npm, Docker, or Kubernetes with a Helm Chart.
