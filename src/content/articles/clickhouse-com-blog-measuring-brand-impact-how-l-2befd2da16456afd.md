For two minutes at a time, whichever model has the best results buys the media, before the model resets and the process begins again.</p>\n<p>For LoopMe’s customers —&#160;PepsiCo Mountain Dew, Sony Pictures, Warner Bros Discovery., to name a few — its value lies in the ability to enhance upper-funnel measurement, focusing on building brand equity rather than just driving clicks.
