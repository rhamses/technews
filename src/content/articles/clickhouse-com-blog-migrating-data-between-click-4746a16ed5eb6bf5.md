The cardinality of the type column is very low - 5 values, meaning 6.3M rows have a <code>flat</code> value out of 34M  ~1/6th of the total dataset. Because <code>type='flat'</code> rows are distributed in all data blocks (around 24 rows per block), the probability of having <code>flat</code> value in any single block is very high (it’s 1/6th with 24 rows per block).
