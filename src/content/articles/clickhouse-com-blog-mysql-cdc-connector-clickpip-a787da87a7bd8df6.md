Whether you're running MySQL 5.7, 8.0, or a <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://clickhouse.com/docs/integrations/clickpipes/mysql/source/generic_maria\">MariaDB</a> variant, the connector goes beyond basic compatibility. This allows it to deliver consistent, reliable replication across environments that may behave quite differently under the hood.</p>\n<h4 id=\"parallel-snapshotting-for-blazing-fast-initial-loads\"></h4>\n<p>For the initial load, we are able to <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://github.com/PeerDB-io/peerdb/pull/2909\">optimize performance</a> by partitioning data using a source column you provide, which enables parallel reads and speeds up large-scale migrations. Compared to single-threaded loads, we've seen 3x faster sync times with 4 threads and up to 8–10x faster with 16 threads on well-provisioned systems. This approach also improves reliability: breaking the load into smaller chunks makes it easier to retry specific segments in case of recoverable errors like network hiccups. It also enhances visibility, letting users track progress more granularly.</p>\n<h4 id=\"reliable-and-intelligent-change-data-capture\"></h4>\n<p>The MySQL CDC connector in ClickPipes is built for reliability and ease of use. We <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://github.com/PeerDB-io/peerdb/pull/2557\">automatically detect and propagate column additions</a>, so your ClickHouse schema stays in sync as your source evolves. With broad data type support—<a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://github.com/PeerDB-io/peerdb/pull/2648\">including vector types</a>—you can replicate modern, complex workloads seamlessly. And even during idle periods, <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://github.com/PeerDB-io/peerdb/pull/3070\">we continuously advance our binary log checkpoint</a>, so resuming from interruptions is fast and efficient.</p>\n<p>As more customers began using the connector in real-world environments, we focused on expanding its capabilities to meet the needs of production-scale systems. Currently, we generate alerts for issues like source connection failures, data type mismatches between MySQL and ClickHouse, and ingestion failures on the ClickHouse side.
