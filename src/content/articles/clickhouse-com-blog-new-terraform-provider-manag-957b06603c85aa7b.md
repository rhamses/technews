This new provider supports ClickHouse instances running on both <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://clickhouse.com/\">ClickHouse Cloud</a> or self hosted.</p>\n<p>We have already seen adoption from many ClickHouse Cloud customers for using Terraform to programmatically automate the configuration and management of their ClickHouse services. This Terraform functionality has been supported with our existing <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://registry.terraform.io/providers/ClickHouse/clickhouse/latest\">Clickhouse Terraform provider</a> for managing and deploying services, private endpoints, and Transparent Data Encryption keys for services. Now customers can extend their Terraform use cases with our <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://registry.terraform.io/providers/ClickHouse/clickhousedbops/latest\">clickhousedbops</a> provider. After defining a direct connection to their clickhouse instance, customers can build databases and then define users and roles with privilege grants on that database’s tables or columns.</p>\n<p><span><img alt=\"Terraform.png\" src=\"https://clickhouse.com/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2FTerraform_5c3b11375a.png&w=2048&q=75\"></span></p>\n<h2 id=\"getting-started-with-the-terraform-provider\"></h2>\n<h3 id=\"step-1-provisioning-a-clickhouse-cloud-service\"></h3>\n<p>Imagine you are an engineering team lead and you are interested in building a new ClickHouse database so your team can store and analyze your business unit’s email and phone log data. This new clickhousedbops provider also has support for our customers who want to use Terraform for managing their self-hosted ClickHouse database users, roles, and grant privileges in their own self hosted cloud or on-prem environment.</p>\n<p>To support these open source ClickHouse deployments, if users have multiple replicas then a “cluster_name” variable (database resource <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://registry.terraform.io/providers/ClickHouse/clickhousedbops/latest/docs/resources/role#optional\">example</a>) can be defined in each of the clickhousedbops resource definitions.
