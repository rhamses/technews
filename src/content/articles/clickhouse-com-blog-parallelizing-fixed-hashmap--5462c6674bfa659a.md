The feature is described briefly in the <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://clickhouse.com/blog/clickhouse-release-25-11#parallel-merge-for-small-group-by\">25.11 release post</a>.  ￼</p>\n<p>What follows is the original engineering write-up by <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://github.com/incfly\">Jianfei Hu</a>, who implemented this optimization.
