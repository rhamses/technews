In ClickHouse, each insert creates a new <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://clickhouse.com/docs/parts\">table part</a> (an immutable data segment that ClickHouse later merges in the background), and it's recommended to insert large batches of data at once instead of doing small, frequent inserts. The cost is a significantly larger index on disk.
