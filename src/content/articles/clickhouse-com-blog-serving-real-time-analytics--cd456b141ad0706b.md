To start with, they focus on specific marketplace problems, devise data solutions, and subsequently expand and scale to other marketplaces. This presents a complex challenge, as they need to constantly consider aspects such as reusability, uptime, and scalability.</p>\n<p>To meet the needs of their sellers, Adevinta required a <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://clickhouse.com/resources/engineering/what-is-real-time-analytics\">user-facing real-time analytics</a> and dashboarding solution that would allow the sellers to monitor their advertisements in real-time. However, when Adevinta planned to migrate all its applications to the cloud, the team evaluated various solutions to determine the best fit for their needs.</p>\n<p><span><img alt=\"Adevinta image 2.jpg\" src=\"https://clickhouse.com/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2FAdevinta_image_2_8a1904294d.jpg&w=2048&q=75\"></span>\n<em>Adevinta's data pipeline utilizes Apache Beam Data Flow, running on Google DataFlow, in conjunction with Google Cloud Pub Sub event bus. The data is accessed through a Java client for querying.</em></p>\n<h2 id=\"moving-to-the-cloud\"></h2>\n<p>Adevinta evaluated several cloud-based database services, including ClickHouse Cloud, Google BigQuery, and Cloud Spanner.</p>\n<p>Their main requirements were:</p>\n<ul>\n<li>Fully fledged database service - performant, efficient, has capabilities like indexing, disaster recovery, backup and restore etc.</li>\n<li>Low operational complexity - no site reliability engineer (SRE) required, schema evolution</li>\n<li>Managed service and easily scalable</li>\n<li>Cloud agnostic - nice to have</li>\n<li>Easy to deploy and operate - admin user interface (UI), billing credits etc</li>\n<li>Rich query language</li>\n<li>Low latency, high throughput use case with &lt;3 sec response time as the service level agreement (SLA)</li>\n<li>Current production workload - 80B rows (18TBs)</li>\n<li>Highly analytical queries with SQL interface</li>\n</ul>\n<p>As part of the evaluation, they needed to consider their typical workload. Some workloads require the processing of small query volumes over vast amounts of data, and specialized products excel at that. At the other end of the spectrum, traditional database systems perform well for handling lots of queries over small amounts of data. However, Adevinta’s analytics workload was more analytical in nature, with dozens to hundreds of requests per second, and fell somewhere in between.</p>\n<p>The team found that ClickHouse performed exceptionally well for their specific needs, as it was performant, cloud-agnostic, and more cost-effective than the other solutions. In comparison, BigQuery was 2x more expensive due to its pricing model that charges based on bytes scanned, and Cloud Spanner was 6x more expensive for the workload they tested. Adevinta chose ClickHouse Cloud as the winner, as it fit within their budget and offered the most value for their needs.</p>\n<p>The solution was tested across multiple marketplaces with 22 queries per second, using a single table of 20 billion rows and 20 terabytes of data. You are not leveraging the potential of ClickHouse if you are not using not using ClickHouse Cloud”, remarked the Ad-Growth team.</p>\n<p>The Ad-Growth team also mentioned how ClickHouse is well-suited for scaling analytics solutions to multiple marketplaces within Adevinta. “For instance, think of doing all the deep dive analytics, or AI and ML - you already have the data pre-prepared. ClickHouse helps us in terms of easy integration and onboarding more data into the database instance. It's extremely easy and super flexible. Marketplaces don't have to spend time figuring out a technology which can do this.”</p>\n<p>Adevinta tested the system using approximately 5x their current workload.
