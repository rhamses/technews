Tiffany treated glass like material science and factory choreography: iridescence from metal oxides, furnaces, teams of cutters selecting and foiling pieces. That is closer to how we build software than the cathedral is: recipes, tooling, taste, throughput, and still a window that has to read from a distance and survive weather.</p>\n<p><span><img alt=\"tiffany-industrial-stained-glass.jpg\" src=\"https://clickhouse.com/_next/image?url=%2Fuploads%2Ftiffany_industrial_stained_glass_caae5c572f.jpg&w=2048&q=75\"></span></p>\n<p>You know Notre Dame.
