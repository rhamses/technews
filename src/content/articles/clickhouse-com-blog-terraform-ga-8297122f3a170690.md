ClickPipes, ClickHouse Cloud’s managed data ingestion platform, is no exception.</p></div><div><p>Today, we’re announcing the general availability of ClickPipes resources in Terraform and endpoints in OpenAPI! This means that you’re unlikely to find something in the ClickPipes UI that isn’t exposed via OpenAPI or Terraform. Unless that something is SSH tunneling, but we’re <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://clickhouse.com/blog/terraform-ga#bookmark=id.uz6whn8nwd37\">working on it</a>!</p>\n<p>Every operation now cleanly
