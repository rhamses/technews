Here are a few motivating examples:</p>\n<ol>\n<li>When you want to maintain a <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slowly_changing_dimension\">Slowly Changing Dimension</a></li>\n<li>Change data capture: apply change sets from other data sources</li>\n<li><code>INSERT</code>, <code>UPDATE</code>, or <code>DELETE</code> data with dynamic matching conditions</li>\n<li><a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://link.springer.com/referenceworkentry/10.1007/978-0-387-39940-9_852\">View Maintenance</a> (where the view is a Delta table)</li>\n<li>GDPR compliance</li>\n</ol>\n<p>In general, Delta Lake merge is ideal for when you want to apply selective changes to a Delta table without rewriting the entire table.</p>\n<h2
