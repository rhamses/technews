Transformers, or ONNX Runtime backend.</strong>\n</blockquote>\n<p>PP-OCRv6 is the latest generation of PaddleOCR’s universal OCR model family. It is designed for real-world text detection and recognition across documents, screenshots, multilingual images, digital displays, industrial labels, and scene text.</p>\n<p><a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://cdn-uploads.huggingface.co/production/uploads/652b2e9166313ebb6197e706/BB9bToA0xHZ8Xu5cvBGx0.jpeg\"><img src=\"https://cdn-uploads.huggingface.co/production/uploads/652b2e9166313ebb6197e706/BB9bToA0xHZ8Xu5cvBGx0.jpeg\" alt=\"ppocrv6_det_vis\"></a></p>\n<p>The model family scales from <strong>1.5M to 34.5M parameters</strong>, with three tiers: <strong>tiny</strong>, <strong>small</strong>, and <strong>medium</strong>. The medium and small tiers support <strong>50 languages</strong>, including Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, and 46 Latin-script languages. Try PP-OCRv6 online quickly: <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://huggingface.co/spaces/PaddlePaddle/PP-OCRv6_Online_Demo\">PP-OCRv6 Online Demo</a>.</p>\n<p><a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://cdn-uploads.huggingface.co/production/uploads/652b2e9166313ebb6197e706/Rwhy6dk3g8xb6eyVFL9R_.jpeg\"><img src=\"https://cdn-uploads.huggingface.co/production/uploads/652b2e9166313ebb6197e706/Rwhy6dk3g8xb6eyVFL9R_.jpeg\" alt=\"ocrv6_models\"></a></p>\n<p>On PaddleOCR’s official in-house multi-scenario OCR benchmarks, <strong>PP-OCRv6_medium</strong> reaches <strong>86.2% detection Hmean</strong> and <strong>83.2% recognition accuracy</strong>. Compared with PP-OCRv5_server, it improves text detection by <strong>+4.6 percentage points</strong> and text recognition by <strong>+5.1 percentage points</strong>.</p>\n<p><a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://cdn-uploads.huggingface.co/production/uploads/652b2e9166313ebb6197e706/xYqS-wDYHDE7cUQQdkcqD.png\"><img src=\"https://cdn-uploads.huggingface.co/production/uploads/652b2e9166313ebb6197e706/xYqS-wDYHDE7cUQQdkcqD.png\" alt=\"v6acc_opt\"></a></p>\n<p>PP-OCRv6 focuses on a practical OCR need: producing accurate, structured text outputs with small models and flexible deployment options.
