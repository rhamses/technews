His latest project, intheloop.dev, provides a single gateway for developers to stay up to date.</p>\n<p>As a developer, Kevin loves to stay up to date with the latest technology. Within his network, he also finds himself the go-to person to help his colleagues stay up to speed by searching through Twitter, staying active in forums, and searching out different tech communities. He knows this insight will help others stay up to date at scale, so created intheloop.dev.</p>\n<p>Intheloop.dev is a single point of entry for developers to stay up to date on the topics they care about by aggregating feeds from various sources. It serves as an on-ramp to finding the communities you care about, monitor releases and change logs, critical tweets, and blogs of interest.</p>\n<p>Kevin is launching with coverage on three packages - React, Kotlin and Vite. He is also part of the <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://twitter.com/search?q=%23buildinpublic&src=typed_query\">#buildinpublic</a> movement, In The Loop is open-source - you can <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://github.com/kevcodez/intheloop\">check it out on GitHub</a>. Kevin also developed open-source <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://github.com/supabase/gotrue-kt\">Gotrue</a> and <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://github.com/supabase/postgrest-kt\">PostgREST</a> Kotlin clients for Supabase.</p>\n<p>With over seven years of experience working with relational databases, Kevin knows how powerful Postgres is. Kevin is a Firebase user and knows first hand that he can build fast with Firebase. However, straightforward tasks in Postgres like count functionality, foreign key relations and database normalisation are either insanely difficult or impossible to achieve using Firebase. With Supabase, he gets the benefits of Postgres with the development pace of Firebase. Because he uses Supabase as the backend, it will be easy to add more features in future, like user accounts, when he is ready.</p>\n<h2 id=\"supabase-gives-developers-postgres-supapowers\">Supabase gives Developers Postgres Supapowers<a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://supabase.com/blog/In-The-Loop#supabase-gives-developers-postgres-supapowers\">#</a></h2>\n<p>Kevin loves building with Supabase because of SQL and other Postgres features like constraints, views, and materialised views. Using all these features quickly and efficiently is amazing for Kevin when he knows Supabase is just as easy to integrate as Firebase.</p>\n<blockquote><p>Supabase building on top of Postgres is a true superpower for developers. Postgres is already\ninsanely powerful, and having much more functionality out of the box means I can build quickly and\ncontinue to scale</p><div><p><img alt=\"Kevin Grüneberg  - Creator of intheloop.dev avatar\" src=\"https://supabase.com/_next/image?url=%2Fimages%2Fblog%2Favatars%2Fintheloop-kevin.png&w=64&q=75\"></p><figcaption><p>Kevin Grüneberg  - Creator of intheloop.dev</p></figcaption></div></blockquote>\n<p>With Supabase, Kevin can focus on his idea's core rather than worrying about his tech stack.
