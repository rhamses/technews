contentHtml: "<article><div>\n<p>Today we're releasing 7 new email notification templates for Supabase Auth. These security-related emails can be used to notify users when sensitive actions happen on their account to help surface any suspicious activity.</p>\n<p>For example, a user may receive an email that their password was changed, or that their email address was updated to <code>suspicious@example.com</code>.</p>\n<p>To start, we're introducing the following security notification email templates:</p>\n<ul>\n<li><strong>Password changed</strong> - Notify users when their password has changed</li>\n<li><strong>Email address changed</strong> - Notify users when their email address has changed</li>\n<li><strong>Phone number changed</strong> - Notify users when their phone number has changed</li>\n<li><strong>Identity linked</strong> - Notify users when a new identity (e.g.: GitHub) has been linked to their account</li>\n<li><strong>Identity unlinked</strong> - Notify users when an identity (e.g.: GitHub) has been unlinked from their account</li>\n<li><strong>Multi-factor authentication (MFA) method added</strong> - Notify users when a new multi-factor authentication method has been added to their account</li>\n<li><strong>Multi-factor authentication (MFA) method removed</strong> - Notify users when a multi-factor authentication method has been removed from their account</li>\n</ul>\n<p>Each notification includes relevant context depending on the event. For example, the old email when an address changes, the provider name when an identity is linked or unlinked, or the specific MFA method that was modified. This helps users quickly identify whether the action was legitimate.</p>\n<h3 id=\"dashboard\">Dashboard<a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://supabase.com/blog/introducing-seven-new-email-templates-for-auth#dashboard\">#</a></h3>\n<p>As part of this release, we've also taken some time to give the Emails section in the Dashboard a refresh and a dedicated section in the sidebar.
