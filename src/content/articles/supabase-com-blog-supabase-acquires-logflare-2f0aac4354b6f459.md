It includes various tools working together - APIs, Storage, Auth, and CDNs. A broad tech stack needs a unified measurement system to give a birds-eye view of everything happening within your Supabase project.</p>\n<p>This is especially true for our enterprise customers, who need to know that their servers are not just operating, but optimized with alerting the instant anything goes wrong.</p>\n<h3 id=\"analytics\">Analytics<a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://supabase.com/blog/supabase-acquires-logflare#analytics\">#</a></h3>\n<p>While it's not immediately apparent, logs are analytics! Perhaps you'd like to know which of your users are making the most API requests, or downloading the most files, or spending the most time connected to the application?</p>\n<p>With granular logs, this becomes possible and paves the way for an incredible developer experience: managing your API versioning, predicting growth, and managing billing costs.</p>\n<p>Since that first tweet in May 2020, our platform has generated billions of log entries. Logflare isn't just for querying logs, it displays what's happening in realtime, allowing you to debug your systems live.</li>\n<li>Branding. Who doesn't love the vivid green with dark mode? 😎</li>\n</ul>\n<blockquote><p>I really like how Supabase is taking an existing, proven, piece of technology and making it more\naccessible for more users.
