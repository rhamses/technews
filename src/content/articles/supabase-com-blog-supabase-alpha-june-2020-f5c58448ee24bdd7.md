If you use <code>JSON</code> data in Postgres, then we want to make that easy too. More improvements coming soon for this feature!</p>\n<h3 id=\"choose-your-region\">Choose your region<a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://supabase.com/blog/supabase-alpha-june-2020#choose-your-region\">#</a></h3>\n<p>If you noticed a bit of latency on Supabase, it's because your projects were previously set up in Singapore.
