contentHtml: "<article><div><p><em>tl;dr</em> During the next week, we'll be migrating from \"supabase.io\" to \"supabase.com\".</p>\n<p>There are no changes required on your end. Your API URLs will remain on \"supabase.co\", and only our website URLs will change.</p>\n<p>The rest of this post talks a bit about the origin of the name, but there's nothing important to know.</p>\n<p>At the end of 2019 we released a Postgres <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://github.com/supabase/realtime\">Realtime</a> engine on GitHub.\nWe created this in my previous company after migrating from Firebase to Postgres.</p>\n<p>It started gaining traction (if you count \"GitHub stars\" as traction) and so I tapped my friend, <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://twitter.com/AntWilson\">Ant</a>,\non the shoulder to see if he wanted to build an open source company with me (he was building another start up at the time).</p>\n<p>The company we envisaged had Postgres at the core. We searched for every \"dot com\" we could think of with \"base\" in the name - hyperbase,\nsuperbase, suprabase, uberbase. It turns out this is what every database start does. We couldn't find any good \"dot coms\".\nAnd so we moved on to \"dot io\" domains.</p>\n<p>Anybody who knows Ant, knows that he <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://supabase.com/blog/launch-week\">loves memes</a>. While we were brainstorming\nwe found that \"supabase.io\" was available.</p>\n<p>To be honest, this name wasn't a strong contender, but it served a very important purpose - it was extremely \"meme-able\" because\nit sounds similar to Nikki Minaj's <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://youtu.be/4JipHEz53sU\">Super Bass</a>.</p>\n<p>We chose it as a placeholder and found it was, indeed, very funny to send each other Nikki memes while we brainstormed the business.</p>\n<p>Note to anyone starting a business, don't use a placeholder. You'll never change it.</p>\n<p>Luckily for us, the name <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=23325430\">grew on us</a> and now we love it.</p>\n<p>When we joined Y Combinator, they gave this advice:</p>\n<blockquote>\n<p>Except in unusual circumstances (like <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"http://twitch.tv/\">Twitch.tv</a>), if you don't have the .com version of your name,\nyou should probably change it.</p>\n</blockquote>\n<p>We decided to try purchasing <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"http://supabase.com/\">supabase.com</a> from the current owner. The YC forums were full of founders\nrecommending a particular Domain Specialist who would help us negotiate and purchase the domain. The only catch - he would take\na percentage of the purchase price.</p>\n<p>This struck us as odd. The incentives were more aligned to the domain seller than the purchaser.</p>\n<p>We're not afraid to roll up our sleeves at Supabase, so we decided we could handle it ourselves.</p>\n<p>We were in for a huge surprise. After searching on the (old) <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"http://supabase.com/\">supabase.com</a> website, we found a company address.\nAnd it was literally 100m from my home.</p>\n<p>Of all the places in the world that the domain could be registered, the owner was within yelling-distance. But we didn't yell. We simply\nasked, and after some negotiation, the owner agreed to sell it.</p>\n<p>We were always willing to walk away from the Supabase brand if we needed to, but the price was reasonable for both parties. There really\nisn't much more to it than that.</p>\n<p>Over the next few weeks, we'll update our domain name across most of the sites. If you're using a Supabase database and API, you're on\na \".co\" domain so your apps won't be affected at all.</p>\n<p>You may also find some <a target=\"_blank\" href=\"https://twitter.com/AntWilson/status/1354343248098070530\">easter eggs</a> appear ...</p></div></article>"
