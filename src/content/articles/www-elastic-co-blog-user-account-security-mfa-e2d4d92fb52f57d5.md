MFA renders these attacks ineffective as the attacker would lack the additional authentication factor.</span></p></li><li><p><span><strong>Compliance with security standards</strong></span><span><strong>:</strong> Many industry regulations and security frameworks require or strongly recommend MFA, especially for accessing sensitive data or systems.</span></p></li><li><p><span><strong>Early detection of unauthorized access attempts</strong></span><span><strong>:</strong> MFA can alert you to potential security breaches.
