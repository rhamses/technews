The app will enter maintenance mode in <strong>July 2026</strong> and will be fully removed in <strong>November 2026</strong>.</p><p>If you are one of the small number of customers currently using the Native App, your dbt Labs account team will reach out directly to support a smooth transition. For the vast majority of dbt + Snowflake users, this change has no impact on your workflows.</p><h2 id=\"what-this-means-for-you\">What this means for you</h2><h3 id=\"if-you-dont-use-the-native-app\">If you don't use the native app</h3><p>No action is required. No new features will be released.</li><li><strong>Your dbt Labs account team will contact you directly</strong> to walk through your specific situation and discuss your options. If you do not hear from your account team, please email <a target=\"_blank\" title=\"support@dbtlabs.com\" href=\"mailto:support@dbtlabs.com\">support@dbtlabs.com</a> by <strong>July 2026</strong>.</li><li><strong>After November 2026</strong>, the app will be fully delisted from the Snowflake Marketplace, and access will be discontinued.</li></ul><h2 id=\"why-were-making-this-change\">Why we're making this change</h2><p>When we originally launched the dbt Snowflake Native App, our goal was to bring dbt-powered AI capabilities directly into the Snowflake environment. Since then, the ways dbt and Snowflake work together have evolved significantly — and customers now have access to better-supported, more capable options.</p><ul><li><strong>dbt’s Semantic Layer is available in Snowflake today — without the Native App.</strong> Customers can model governed metrics in dbt and use them in Snowflake through Snowflake Native integrations (e.g., Semantic Views / Snowflake Intelligence) with dbt providing the trusted semantic definitions behind the experience. If you were using the Native App to bring dbt context into Snowflake, these options deliver a deeper, more reliable integration.</li><li><strong>The AI landscape has matured.</strong> When the Native App launched, bringing a dbt-powered chatbot into Snowflake was a reasonable experiment. Today, Snowflake Intelligence and Cortex provide a far richer AI experience — with dbt's Semantic Layer powering the trusted context behind them.</li><li><strong>We want to focus investment where it benefits you most.</strong> Rather than maintaining an app that hasn't kept pace, we're directing our partnership efforts toward the integrations that customers are actually adopting and finding valuable — like Semantic Views, OSI, and native dbt execution on Snowflake.</li></ul><p>dbt will continue to support Snowflake data teams with interoperable products and strong integration. We’ll also continue to help teams deliver transformations while providing observability into AI agentic data operations — so customers can build and operate trusted, governed data products that power analytics and AI on Snowflake.</p><div><table><thead><tr><th>Milestone</th><th>Date</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><p>Annoucement</p></td><td><p>Now</p></td></tr><tr><td><p>New installations disabled</p></td><td><p>Now</p></td></tr><tr><td><p>Maintenance mode begins (critical fixes only)</p></td><td><p>Now</p></td></tr><tr><td><p>Final removal and marketplace delisting</p></td><td><p>November 2026</p></td></tr></tbody></table></div><h2 id=\"support-and-feedback\">Support and feedback</h2><p>If you have questions about this change or need help transitioning:</p><ul><li><strong>Email:</strong> <a target=\"_blank\" title=\"support@dbtlabs.com\" href=\"mailto:support@dbtlabs.com\">support@dbtlabs.com</a></li><li><strong>Your account team:</strong> Your CSM or account executive can help with your specific situation</li></ul><p>We welcome feedback on the timeline.
